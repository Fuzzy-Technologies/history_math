What is harmony like? Solve the problem and find out:

“Beyond the looking glass live creatures with many heads, arms and legs. A creature is clever if it has more heads than arms and legs combined; strong if it has more arms than heads and legs combined; and quick if it has more legs than heads and arms combined. Can any creature be all three: clever, strong and quick?”

## Solution

Let $H$ count heads, $A$ arms and $L$ legs. We obtain:

$H>A+L$.<br>
$A>H+L$.<br>
$L>H+A$.

Add the inequalities term by term:

$H+A+L>2(H+A+L)$.

Thus $H+A+L<0$. If $H,A,L$ are all nonnegative, that is impossible.

But this is the world beyond the looking glass! Set $H=-3$, $A=-4$, $L=-5$.

Then everything works: $-3>-9$, $-4>-8$, $-5>-7$.

Only joking!

## Notes

A. V. Zhukov published this problem in [“Monsters Beyond the Looking Glass”](https://www.kvant.digital/issues/1996/4/zhukov-monstryi_zazerkalya-bb6c8254/) (*Kvant*, 1996, no. 4, p. 26, in Russian).

The problem and solution also appear in his *Elegantnaya matematika: Zadachi i resheniya* (Elegant Mathematics: Problems and Solutions), Moscow: LIBROKOM, 2016, pp. 53, 109–110 (in Russian).
