Mikhail Vasilyevich Ostrogradsky (1801–1862), the well-known Russian mathematician, disliked following fashion. A tailor made him a new suit. “I’ve made everything in the latest style,” he assured him. “You mustn’t fall behind the times!” “But how am I supposed to keep up with the times in trousers this tight?!” Ostrogradsky asked, quite reasonably.
