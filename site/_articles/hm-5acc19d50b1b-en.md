*Marking the 120th anniversary of his birth*

“We Could Use a Sea of Laptevs” was the title of an article about him in a newspaper published across the republic—a play on the name of the Laptev Sea.

Boris Lukich Laptev was a distinguished Soviet mathematician and a leading representative of the Kazan school of geometry. He held a Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences degree and was a professor. He received the P. L. Chebyshev Gold Medal in 1984 and was an Honoured Scientist of the RSFSR from 1975 and of the Tatar Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic from 1965.

His entire research, teaching and public career was connected with Kazan University.

## Education and Geometrical Research

He was born on 23 April 1905 in Kazan. He graduated from Kazan School No. 24 and entered the Mathematics Division of Kazan University’s Faculty of Physics and Mathematics in 1923.

He spoke French fluently, studied Latin and read German, English and Italian.

He graduated from the university in 1930 and remained on the faculty as an assistant.

He began research in differential geometry under Pyotr Alekseyevich Shirokov. In 1939 he defended his Candidate of Sciences dissertation, “Studies in the Geometry of Finsler Spaces”, and in 1959 his doctoral dissertation, “The Space of Supporting Elements”.

Laptev was one of the pioneers of research in the differential geometry of generalized spaces. In 1984 the USSR Academy of Sciences awarded him the P. L. Chebyshev Medal for a series of works on the geometry of generalized spaces.

From 1944 to 1961 he headed the Department of Geometry and served as dean of the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics. From 1961 to 1980 he directed the N. G. Chebotaryov Research Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics.

## Mathematical History and Lobachevsky’s Legacy

Laptev was also widely known for research in the history of mathematics, especially the history of Kazan University. He wrote a scholarly biography of N. I. Lobachevsky for an anniversary collection in 1943. From 1948, together with A. P. Norden, he actively helped prepare and publish Lobachevsky’s collected works. As editor-in-chief together with P. S. Alexandrov, he completed this enormous undertaking by preparing the supplementary volume *N. I. Lobachevsky: Scientific and Pedagogical Heritage, University Leadership, Fragments, Letters* in 1976. He organized and led the Lobachevsky Readings at Kazan State University.

He also helped create the filmstrip *N. I. Lobachevsky* and the film *The Mystery of the Fifth Postulate*. Devoted to popularizing Lobachevsky’s ideas, he wrote books for a broad audience, including *Nikolai Ivanovich Lobachevsky* (1976) and *N. I. Lobachevsky and His Geometry* (1976). These drew on his extensive experience of lecturing on the subject to school pupils.

He authored and co-authored books and articles on the lives and work of A. P. Kotelnikov, P. A. Shirokov, A. P. Norden, V. I. Shulikovsky, V. V. Wagner, B. M. Gagaev, I. P. Egorov and K. P. Persidsky.

A man of great culture and wide learning, Laptev had his own scale of values, inherited from another time, when talent and knowledge were considered the most important qualities a person could possess.

## Notes

Laptev wrote extensively and engagingly about the history of Kazan University:

- Laptev, B. L. “Mathematical Life in Kazan during the Great Patriotic War”. *Izvestiya Vysshikh Uchebnykh Zavedenii. Matematika*, 1985, no. 5, pp. 3–6.

- Laptev, B. L. “Mathematics at Kazan University in the Soviet Period”. *Scholarly Notes of Kazan University*, 120:7, 1960, pp. 24–66.

- Laptev, B. L. “Geometrical Research at Kazan University: Marking the Sixtieth Anniversary of the USSR”. *Izvestiya Vysshikh Uchebnykh Zavedenii. Matematika*, 1982, no. 12, pp. 3–10.

- Laptev, B. L. “Mathematics at Kazan University over 175 Years, 1804–1979”. *Izvestiya Vysshikh Uchebnykh Zavedenii. Matematika*, 1979, no. 10, pp. 3–13.

- Laptev, B. L. “Mathematics at Kazan University over Sixty Years”. *Izvestiya Vysshikh Uchebnykh Zavedenii. Matematika*, 1977, no. 10, pp. 3–13; English translation: “Mathematics at Kazan University during the Last Sixty Years”, *Soviet Mathematics (Izvestiya VUZ)*, 21:10, 1977, pp. 1–8.

- Laptev, B. L. “Mathematics at Kazan University over Fifty Years”. *Izvestiya Vysshikh Uchebnykh Zavedenii. Matematika*, 1967, no. 10, pp. 3–10.
