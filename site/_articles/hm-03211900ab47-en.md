*Marking the 100th anniversary of his birth*

A Soviet mathematics educator and author of books on extracurricular activities.

He was born on 8 October 1924 in Lyubim, Yaroslavl Region.

He held a Candidate of Pedagogical Sciences degree and the academic rank of docent. A graduate of the Perm State Pedagogical Institute, he taught there for almost forty years. He headed the Department of Elementary Mathematics in 1967–1975 and served as dean of the Faculty of Mathematics in 1975–1985.

He helped organize mathematics education beyond the classroom in Perm Region and developed methods for extracurricular mathematics activities.

In his well-known book *Games for a Mathematics Circle* (in Russian, 1972), he shows how games can be used in extracurricular mathematics activities with schoolchildren. The book offers a range of engaging mathematical games: competitions, mazes, Mathematical Trackers, the Mathematical Train, the Mathematical Rally, and board games and games involving physical activity. It also gives advice on organizing and running them.
