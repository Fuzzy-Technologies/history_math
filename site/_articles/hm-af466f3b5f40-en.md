*Marking the 90th anniversary of his birth*

A Soviet and Russian mathematician and educator. He became a full member of the Russian Academy of Education in 1993, received the degree of Doctor of Pedagogical Sciences in 1985 and became a professor in 1986.

## Education and Research

He was born on 19 March 1934 in Novograd-Volynsky, Ukrainian SSR.

He graduated from Kursk Pedagogical Institute in 1955. From 1958 he taught mathematics in Moscow schools and higher education institutions, and from 1961 he worked within the Academy of Pedagogical Sciences.

In 1966 he defended his Candidate of Sciences dissertation, “The Relationship Between Geometry Teaching and Pupils’ Life Experience in an Eight-Year Evening (Shift) School”. In 1984 he defended his doctoral dissertation, “Methods for Forming and Developing Adults’ Spatial Understanding Through School Geometry Teaching”. The Russian dissertation titles are translated here.

From 1968 he headed the laboratory for evening-school teaching at the Research Institute of Adult Education.

From 1986 he was a professor at the V. I. Lenin Moscow State Pedagogical Institute. From 1993 he was academician-secretary of the Division of General Secondary Education of the Russian Academy of Education, and in 1996–2001 he directed its Institute for the Informatization of Education.

## Teaching Materials and an Anthology

His principal work concerned mathematics, didactics, mathematics teaching methods, and the content and organization of adult education. He developed coordinated sets of teaching materials in geometry, algebra and mathematical analysis for secondary schools, aligned with Russia’s 2010 Federal State Educational Standards.

One book he compiled is particularly interesting and useful:

*Mathematics: An Anthology on History, Methodology and Teaching* (Russian title translated here), compiled by G. D. Gleizer. Moscow: URAO Publishing House, 2001. 384 pp.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-2" %}

The anthology contains articles and extracts from books by outstanding scholars on the nature and significance of mathematics, its history, methodology and teaching. They explore mathematics’ role in the modern world as a means of solving applied problems, cultivating mathematical culture and developing an interest in studying mathematics.
