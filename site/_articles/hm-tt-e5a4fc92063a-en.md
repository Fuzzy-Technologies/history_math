> “Whether you think you can, or you think you cannot, you are right.”  
> Henry Ford

Henry Ford was an exceptionally remarkable person: an American industrialist and business magnate who founded a company bearing his own name, the Henry Ford Company.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-2" %}

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-3" %}

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-4" %}

Ford reduced the time needed to assemble a car on a production line from 12 hours to 90 minutes. This brought prices down, eventually making cars accessible to the middle classes.

Ford introduced an eight-hour working day in his factories and a system of rewards for a sober way of life. People queued for jobs with him. In hiring, he valued only willingness to work. Henry also preferred people with a mathematical — or more precisely, logical and creative — turn of mind.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-5" %}

There is a legend about how he recruited engineers. At an interview for an engineering job at Ford's company, candidates were given a cryptarithm: DONALD + GERALD = ROBERT.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-6" %}

Identical letters represent identical digits; different letters represent different digits. Candidates were given a head start: D = 5. Solving it in 15 minutes gave them a good chance of a job. Another version says: solve it in 20 minutes and become an engineer; in 25 minutes, a technician; in 40 minutes, an assembly-line worker.

After two years in which nobody solved the puzzle within 15 minutes, Ford published it in newspapers as a curiosity.

Can you solve it? How long does it take you?

## Solution {#section-1}

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-9" %}

There are many solution algorithms. One is described below. Solving the puzzle involves quite a few trial assumptions. To keep the account short, we follow the correct chain of reasoning directly.

1) Since D = 5, adding D + D gives 10. Thus T = 0, with 1 carried into the tens column.

2) Since D(= 5) + G = R, R > 6. R is also odd, because the tens column gives 2L + 1 = R. Therefore R = 7 or R = 9.

3) Since О + E = О, E = 9, so R = 7. Then G = 1 or G = 2, because the sum О + E is not yet known.

4) 2L + 1 = 7 or = 17. Therefore L = 3 or L = 8. L = 3 does not work, since 2A = E = 9. Hence L = 8.

5) 2A + 1 = 9, so A = 4.

6) We need N + 7 > 9. Only N = 6 fits. Therefore B = 3.

7) The digits 0, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 have been used. The only possibility for O is O = 2. Then G = 1.

The puzzle is solved.
