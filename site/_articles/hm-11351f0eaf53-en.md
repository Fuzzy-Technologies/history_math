Professor E. S. Venttsel recalled that, as a mathematics student at Leningrad University, she had heard her teachers describe the famous lecture on cutting clothes that P. L. Chebyshev gave in Paris in 1878. In Venttsel’s telling, many of the listeners were tailors, and Chebyshev opened his talk with a memorable line: “For simplicity, ladies and gentlemen, let us assume that the human body is a sphere!”

P.S. We have already [mentioned](https://t.me/history_math/210) this curious incident in our article about the French curve, a tool familiar to every tailor. Read about it in [The French Curve](https://teletype.in/@history_math/Lekalo-02-11).
