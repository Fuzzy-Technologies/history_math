## Historical background

In *Discourse on the Method* (*Discours de la méthode*, 1637), [René Descartes](https://t.me/history_math/52) (Latin: Renatus Cartesius, 1596–1650) wrote in French: “Je pense, donc je suis” (“I think, therefore I am”).

In the Second Meditation of *Meditations on First Philosophy* (*Meditationes de prima philosophia*, 1641), the proposition appears as “Ego sum, ego existo” (“I am, I exist”), while the Latin “Cogito, ergo sum” appears in *Principles of Philosophy* (*Principia philosophiae*, 1644).

This is the central proposition of his philosophy: thinking is the first and indisputable proof of the subject’s existence. Even if everything else may be an illusion, the very act of doubting confirms the existence of someone who doubts.
