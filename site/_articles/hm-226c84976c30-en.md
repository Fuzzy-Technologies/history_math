Today’s joke is an entirely true story.

When my son and I were working on our dissertations for the Candidate of Sciences degree, we hung a poster on the wall with a line from another dissertation. Read every word carefully, over and over again, and soon you’ll have flashes of mathematical inspiration. I can vouch for it!

“The fractal dimension of a strange attractor in phase space and the correlation integral can be chosen as quantities to monitor in dynamical systems.”
