The [Bourbaki group](https://teletype.in/@history_math/Burbaki-04-06), the collective that wanted to rewrite mathematics from scratch with the utmost rigour, had an almost theatrical custom.

An active member was expected to retire at about fifty. The idea was both simple and harsh: Bourbaki should remain the work of young mathematicians, rather than a club of distinguished veterans. Arnold’s humorous story, however, adds a special test known as the “coconutization procedure”.

According to Arnold’s story, the name came from a tale about a chief whose fitness to lead was tested by making him climb a coconut palm. Hold on, and he was still fit for the job. Fall, and it was time for a replacement.

Bourbaki’s version was, of course, mathematical.

A member turning fifty was invited to the next seminar as usual. Halfway through the talk, when the audience was beginning to nod off, the speaker, who was in on the game, would insert a lengthy, deliberately obscure definition. At that moment, the person being tested was supposed to perk up and say something like:

“Excuse me, but only the empty set satisfies your definition!”

Noticing this meant that his mathematical instinct was still intact, and he remained in the ranks. If he failed to notice, nobody made a scene: the next invitation might simply never arrive.

The joke is splendid in itself: in ordinary life, people are judged by age, experience or honours; in Bourbaki, by their ability to stay awake through a long lecture.

And that is, perhaps, very Bourbakist.

Further information and primary sources:

- Armand Borel, [“Twenty-Five Years with Nicolas Bourbaki, 1949–1973”](https://www.ams.org/notices/199803/borel.pdf): recollections of a former member, explicitly stating that retirement was to be at about fifty and that the rule was strictly enforced from 1956 (PDF, in English).

- Boris Khesin, [“Memories of Vladimir Arnold”](https://www.essra.org.cn/upload/202008/132419392399818989.pdf): a retelling of Arnold’s coconutization story, with the coconut palm, half-page definition and remark about the empty set (PDF, in English).

- [An overview of Bourbaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Bourbaki), noting that the age-fifty rule was a group custom and was not always formally documented.
