Speaking at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Moscow, the American founder of dynamic programming, Richard Bellman, a great joker, caused a stir in the audience. He wrote “Can a machine think?” on the board and proceeded to give it a perfectly serious analysis. He argued that we were not yet able to define what “can” and “think” meant, and that the only thing we understood in the whole sentence was the question mark.
