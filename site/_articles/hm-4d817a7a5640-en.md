Mathematicians are joking, and the channel’s editor feels a little offended 🤨

The telephone rings in the mathematics faculty’s dean’s office. A deputy dean from the Department of Mathematical Analysis answers.

Caller: How do you construct a $50^\circ$ angle?<br>
Covering the receiver with a hand, the deputy dean begins to think: “$50^\circ$ is somewhere around one radian…” Then he starts trying to remember things about $\pi$ and the circumference of a circle. Another deputy dean, from the geometry department, is sitting nearby. He says it cannot be constructed with a straightedge and compass.<br>
Just then the dean enters. They put the question to him. He takes the phone and asks, “Who’s calling?”<br>
Caller: The Faculty of Philology.<br>
Dean: Use a protractor!
