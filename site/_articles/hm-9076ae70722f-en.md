Mathematicians joke and put things in perspective.

In the early 1940s, an American schoolgirl complained to Einstein that she was having great difficulty with mathematics. With his characteristic irony, he replied:

“Do not worry about your difficulties in mathematics. I can assure you mine are still greater.”
