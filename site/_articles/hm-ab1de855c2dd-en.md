[Vladimir Vladimirovich Morozov](https://t.me/history_math/83) (1910–1975) was a professor at Kazan University, an algebraist and the long-serving head of its Department of Algebra. Even in exam stories, his style is instantly recognisable.

Once, Morozov was examining a rather weak student. The young man answered listlessly and got muddled. Trying to bring him up to a four out of five, the professor kept prompting him:

“What if you think again?”

But it did no good. At last, Morozov sighed:

“I would give your knowledge a three.”

The student immediately came to life and replied:

“What if you think again?”

The professor appreciated his quick wit, smiled and gave him a four after all. His scholarship was saved.

Source: Sergei Fedin, *Mathematicians Joke Too* (in Russian), the story “Wriggled Out of It”, as told by Professor V. V. Skvortsov.
