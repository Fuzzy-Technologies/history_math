Mathematicians are joking about programmers. A risky business, as everyone knows. :)

An algebra class for programmers. The teacher writes on the board: $\sin X=1$.<br>
“Well, who can find $X$?”<br>
A programmer rushes up and says delightedly:<br>
“Here! Here’s $X$!” He points at the $X$.
