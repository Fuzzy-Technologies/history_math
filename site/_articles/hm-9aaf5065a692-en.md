The new academic year has begun, and pupils, teachers and lecturers have their work cut out. Meanwhile, mathematicians are joking.

During exams, a student from Moscow State University’s Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics goes to the library.<br>
“Where’s the librarian?” he asks the cleaner.<br>
“In the archive.”<br>
“Could you unzip him, please? I urgently need a book.”
