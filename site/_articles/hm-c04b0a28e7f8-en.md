An analytic geometry lecture is under way at a Moscow university. The lecturer draws a construction in three-dimensional coordinates and asks the customary rhetorical question:

“If this is our $X$, and that is our $Y$, then where is our $Z$?”

A gravelly voice comes from the class:

“Zed is dead, baby. Zed is dead.”

…And suddenly you are no longer in a lecture theatre, but on set with Tarantino! 🥷
