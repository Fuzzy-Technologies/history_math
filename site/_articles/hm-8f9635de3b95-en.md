Oleg Borisovich Lupanov (1932–2006) was a mathematician, one of the founders of the Soviet school of discrete mathematics and the long-serving dean of Moscow State University’s Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics. At the faculty, he was said to be almost a constant: generations of students came and went, while stories about the dean kept multiplying.

One favourite is the lift story.

Spring, in the main building of Moscow State University. After class, everyone rushes for the lift, which fills up at once. The dean stands in the far corner, hidden behind broad backs.

Someone runs up from outside, points to the corner and gives a brisk order:

“Move over! There is nobody in that corner!”

Everyone smiles. Then a calm voice emerges from the depths of the lift:

“I am not a nobody! I am your dean!”

The moral: in the faculty lift, an apparently empty space may turn out to be… the faculty administration!
