When someone says, “What is so special about your geometry…”, it is a good moment to recall that, for Mendeleev, it was more than a textbook chapter: it was a starting point for the independent development of science in Russia.

“Geometrical knowledge forms the foundation of all exact science, and the originality of Lobachevsky’s geometry marks the dawn of the independent development of science in Russia. The seeds of science will yield a harvest for the people.”<br>
— Dmitri Mendeleev, honorary member of Kazan University
