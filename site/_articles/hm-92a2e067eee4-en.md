Everyone knows the saying, “If you’re so clever, why are you so poor?” Here is a rigorous mathematical explanation of this phenomenon.

Postulate 1: $\text{Knowledge}=\text{Force}$ (a literal twist on “knowledge is power”).

Postulate 2: $\text{Time}=\text{Money}$

Every schoolchild knows that:

$$\frac{\text{Work}}{\text{Time}}=\text{Force}\cdot\text{Speed}\tag{1}$$

Substituting the two postulates into (1), we obtain:

$$\frac{\text{Work}}{\text{Money}}=\text{Knowledge}\cdot\text{Speed}\tag{2}$$

Rearranging gives:

$$\frac{\text{Work}}{\text{Knowledge}\cdot\text{Speed}}=\text{Money}\tag{3}$$

Equation (3) shows that if we let Knowledge or Speed tend to zero, we receive infinite Money for any amount of Work.

Conclusion: the more stupid and lazy a person is, the more money they can earn.

Mathematicians are joking when they should be earning money!
