Find the area of a right triangle whose hypotenuse is 6 and whose altitude to the hypotenuse is 4. The answer 12 is incorrect.

P.S. This is a well-known problem which, according to V. I. Arnold, Soviet schoolchildren could solve but American schoolchildren could not. What is the catch?

## Solution

The problem has no solution, since the altitude cannot exceed half the hypotenuse, which is the radius of the circumcircle.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-2" %}

Historical background. Vladimir Igorevich Arnold (1937–2010) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician who worked in topology, differential equations and theoretical mechanics. One of the leading mathematicians of the twentieth century, he was a full member of the USSR Academy of Sciences and a foreign associate of the US National Academy of Sciences.
