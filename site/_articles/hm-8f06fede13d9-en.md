A mathematician is being admitted to an aristocratic English club. The secretary, as dry as a set of minutes, explains the rules with the utmost formality:

“First, never speak of anything you do not know for certain.<br>
Second, do not judge the whole club by just a few of its members.<br>
And third, do not attach undue importance to trifles.”

The mathematician sighs, looks at her as though she were a badly worded problem, and asks quietly:

“Ah… so it is farewell to asymptotic methods?”

## Notes

Source of the joke. The Russian word *chlen* can mean both a member of a club and a term of a series.

- S. N. Fedin, [*Mathematicians Joke Too*](https://www.docdroid.net/ytC3FTI/fedin-sn-matematiki-toze-sutiat-pdf.pdf) (in Russian), anecdote no. 93, “Conditions of Admission”.
