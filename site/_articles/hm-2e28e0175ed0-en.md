Nicole Oresme (1323–1382) was a French mathematician and scholar of mechanics. He was also a philosopher, theologian and bishop. His scientific writings influenced Copernicus, Galileo and Descartes.

One day Oresme decided to explain the concept of infinity to a philosopher friend. He began halving a line segment, then halving it again, and so on. “When will this end?” the philosopher asked. “Never. That is what infinity is!” Oresme replied.
