People once counted on their fingers without embarrassment: their hands were a great help in calculating. Fingers can serve as a mathematical tool.

Let us look at a few magical tricks of arithmetic — the old name for the science and practice of calculating with numbers.

Here is the rule for multiplying by 9 on your fingers. Do this:

Count your fingers from the left and bend the one whose number you want to multiply by 9.

$1\times9=9$ (bend the first finger; nine fingers remain to its right, so the answer is 9).<br>
$2\times9=18$ (bend the second finger; one finger comes before it and eight after it, so the answer is 18).<br>
$3\times9=27$ (bend the third finger; two fingers come before it and seven after it, so the answer is 27).<br>
…<br>
$7\times9=63$ (bend the seventh finger; six fingers come before it and three after it, so the answer is 63).<br>
…<br>
And so on — see the diagram.

Such little tricks or useful tips are known in slang as “lifehacks”, combining “life” with “hacking”, a term from computing. Similar Russian words mean ingenuity, a recipe or a clever discovery. We will keep sharing mathematical tricks like these in our posts.
