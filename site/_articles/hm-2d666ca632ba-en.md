The Italian polymath [Leonardo da Vinci](https://t.me/history_math/29) (1452–1519), celebrated as an artist, engineer and inventor, saw mathematics as a model of scientific proof.

One day he decided to grow a square watermelon to make it easier to store. After several unsuccessful attempts, he wrote in his notes: “Nature does not like right angles, but I’m not giving up!”

P.S. Read more about Leonardo da Vinci’s mathematical discoveries—and his other discoveries too—in [Da Vinci’s Fountain of Ideas](https://teletype.in/@history_math/Fontan-idej-da-Vinchi-08-31) (in Russian).
