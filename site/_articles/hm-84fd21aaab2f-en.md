In the 1957 edition of *A Mathematician’s Miscellany*, the English mathematician John Littlewood (1885–1977) recounted many amusing incidents from his life. Here is one, translated from V. I. Levin’s Russian edition:

“A memorandum I wrote for the Ballistics Directorate around 1917 ended with the sentence ‘Thus, $\sigma$ should be made as small as possible.’ In the printed memorandum, the sentence was missing. But P. J. Grigg said, ‘What is this?’ A barely visible speck in the blank space at the end turned out to be the tiniest $\sigma$ I had ever seen. The typesetters must have searched all London for it.”
