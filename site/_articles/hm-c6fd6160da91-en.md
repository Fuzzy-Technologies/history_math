How can 5 apples be divided equally among six children without cutting any apple into 6 or more pieces?

## Solution

🍏🍏🍏🍎🍎

🍏🍏🍏 Cut three apples into halves,<br>
🍎🍎 and the other two into three equal pieces each.

Each child should receive half an apple and one third of an apple.
