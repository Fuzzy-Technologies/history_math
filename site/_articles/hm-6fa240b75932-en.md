Three little girls, Mary, Nellie and Susie, collected 770 chestnuts and divided them in proportion to their ages. For every 4 chestnuts Mary took, Nellie took 3; and for every 6 Mary received, Susie received 7. How many did each girl get?

## Solution

The way the chestnuts were divided tells us only that the girls’ ages, from youngest to oldest, are in the ratio $9:12:14$. The youngest therefore received 198 chestnuts, the middle girl 264, and the eldest 308. Their ages themselves cannot be determined uniquely.
