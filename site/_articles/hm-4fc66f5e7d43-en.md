Mathematicians joke and feel a little sorry for mathematics professors.

A professor arrived in a lecture room to find just three students. There was nothing for it: he went to the blackboard and began his lecture. After a while, as he was writing on the board, five students quietly slipped out of the room.

The professor turned round and thought bitterly, “Just my luck. Two more will come in, and there’ll be nobody left at all!”
