A village wine merchant called his three sons together and told them to divide equally among themselves 7 full barrels of wine, 7 identical barrels half full of wine, and 7 more identical barrels that were empty.

How can the sons divide the wine and the barrels so that each receives both the same amount of wine and the same number of barrels, if wine must not be poured from one barrel into another?

## Solution

The first and second sons should each receive 2 full barrels, 3 half-full barrels and 2 empty barrels. The third son should receive 3 full barrels, 1 half-full barrel and 3 empty barrels.
