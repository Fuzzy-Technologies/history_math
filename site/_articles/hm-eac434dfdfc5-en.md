When Jacques Sturm (1803–1855) came to his famous theorem for counting the real roots of a polynomial in his lectures, he would turn to his audience and say: “And now, gentlemen, I shall present the theorem whose name I have the honour to bear!”

## Notes

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-2" %}

Jacques Charles François Sturm (1803–1855) was a French mathematician, a professor at the École Polytechnique, a member of the Paris Academy of Sciences and a foreign member of the Royal Society of London. He received a mathematics prize for his work on the compressibility of liquids.

Principal works:

- Memoir on the Solution of Numerical Equations (*Mémoire sur la résolution des équations numériques*), presented in 1829 and published in full in 1835
- Course of Analysis (*Cours d’analyse de l’École Polytechnique*, 1857–1863)
- Course of Mechanics (*Cours de mécanique de l’École Polytechnique*, 1861)
