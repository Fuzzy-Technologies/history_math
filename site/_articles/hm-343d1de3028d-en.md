Any number of people can play.

The leader tips a handful of coins onto a table. Turning away, they ask the players to turn over any coins as often as they like, but always two at a time.

A player then covers any one coin with a hand.

The leader turns back and says whether the hidden coin is heads or tails — the emblem side or the reverse.

How is it done? What is the game’s secret?

## How the trick works

Before turning away, the leader remembers whether the number of heads or tails is even or odd. Turning over two coins preserves that parity. The side whose visible count has changed parity is facing up on the covered coin.
