Grigori Perelman proved the Poincaré conjecture. In 2006, he declined the Fields Medal. In 2010, the Clay Mathematics Institute awarded him the Millennium Prize for his proof of the Poincaré conjecture; Perelman declined its one-million-dollar monetary award. He is known both for his genius and for his extreme reluctance to engage with publicity.

In 2006, journalists tried to interview him at his St Petersburg flat. He opened the door, gestured towards the corridor and said:

“You won’t understand a thing.”

Then he closed the door.

He later commented on his refusal of awards:

“I know how to control the universe. Why would I chase a million?”
