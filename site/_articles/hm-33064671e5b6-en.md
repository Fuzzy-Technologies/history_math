A biologist, a physicist and a mathematician are travelling through Australia when they see a black sheep grazing in a meadow.

Biologist:<br>
“Look, black sheep live in Australia.”

Physicist:<br>
“No, at least one black sheep lives in Australia.”

Mathematician:<br>
“No, gentlemen. At least one sheep lives in Australia, and it is black on at least one side.”
