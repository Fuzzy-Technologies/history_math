Can you count on your fingers? Now let us do it differently.

| Finger | Numbers          |
| ------ | ---------------- |
| Thumb  | 1, 9, 17         |
| Index  | 2, 8, 10, 16, 18 |
| Middle | 3, 7, 11, 15, 19 |
| Ring   | 4, 6, 12, 14, 20 |
| Little | 5, 13, 21, …     |

Which finger will you end on if you count this way up to 2024?

What experiment did you try? What pattern did you discover?

## Answer

The index finger. Notice the repeating cycle of length 8.
