I visited the [Central House of Scientists](https://cduran.ru/) for the first time.

The custodians of Russian scientific traditions and schools of innovation include not only the [Academy of Sciences](https://teletype.in/Matematika-v-Rossijskoj-akademii-nauk-RAN-11-30), its institutes and universities, but also the Houses of Scientists.

The Central House of Scientists is a scientific and cultural institution of the Russian Academy of Sciences. Its address is 16 Prechistenka Street, Moscow, near Kropotkinskaya metro station.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-2" %}

The building is more than 100 years old. The House was established in 1922, and its location on Prechistenka was no accident: the district contains many research institutes, higher-education institutions, Academy of Sciences institutes, the Academy of Arts, Moscow University's humanities faculties, the Russian State Library and numerous museums.

It is a scientific and cultural centre where scientists and engineers meet and relax, exchange scientific information and popularize science. It also hosts meetings with figures from government and culture, musical concerts and literary evenings. It has subject sections, a spoken-word studio, a running enthusiasts' club and two concert halls.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-3" %}

The House currently has 27 scientific sections. I was especially interested in the Mathematics Section.

• Chair: S. S. Demidov, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, professor.

• Deputy chair: V. M. Tikhomirov, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, professor.

• Scientific secretary: A. N. Bogdanov, Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-4" %}

The section meets on the third Thursday of each month in the House's Blue Drawing Room, usually at 18.30. Its meetings often consider the history and methodology of mathematics and mechanics.

The meeting I attended on 21 March 2024 belonged to the series “In Memory of Outstanding Scientists”. It was devoted to the outstanding scholar Yu. I. Manin.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-5" %}

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-6" %}

> Yuri Ivanovich Manin (1937–2023) was a Soviet, later Russian–German, mathematician and educator.  
> One of the founders of noncommutative algebraic geometry, quantum computing theory and quantum information science.  
> Corresponding member of the Russian Academy of Sciences (1991; of the USSR Academy of Sciences from 1990).  
> Member of the Göttingen Academy of Sciences, the Leopoldina, the French Academy of Sciences and the American Academy of Arts and Sciences.  
> Honorary doctor of the Sorbonne, the University of Oslo and the University of Warwick.

Meetings of the scientific sections consider the latest problems at the intersection of different sciences. For example, the joint meeting of the Mathematics Section and the Economic Management Section on 18 April 2024 was entitled “Mathematical Economics: The 21st Century”.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-7" %}

**English key to the slides:**

The slides read:

“Meeting of the Mathematics Section and the Economic Management Section: ‘Mathematical Economics: The 21st Century’.”

“Promising directions for the use of mathematical methods in economics.” A. R. Bakhtizin, CEMI RAS, MSU. Moscow, 18 April 2024.

“Main applications of AI in economics”: (1) neural networks; (2) expert systems; (3) fuzzy logic; (4) genetic algorithms.

The left diagram is headed “Economic system (CGE model)”. Its labels are “Macro-agent 1”, “Macro-agent 2”, “…”, “Macro-agent N”; “Markets”; “Neural network 1”, “…”, “Neural network N”; and “Micro-level agents”. The iteration labels are “Iteration Q−1”, “Iteration Q”, and “Iteration Q+1”. The side boxes read “Data from the previous step of the iterative process” and “Data passed on to the next step of the iterative process”. Its caption reads “A conceptual view of the interaction between the macro and micro levels”.

The right diagram labels the state as “The state — the set of governments (5)” and the four sectors as “Science and education sector (1)”, “Innovation sector (2)”, “Other branches of the economy (3)”, and “Micro-level agents (4)”. Its caption reads “An aggregated diagram of the relationships between the model’s main components”. The mathematical variables and their superscripts/subscripts retain their source notation.

The formula heading reads “Neural network (three layers; the inner layer has 10 neurons)”.

Anyone may visit the scientific sections and attend most meetings according to the schedule. Come along and explore our country's science, history and culture!
