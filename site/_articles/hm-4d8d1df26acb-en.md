*Marking the 115th anniversary of his birth*

A Soviet mathematics educator and author of mathematics textbooks, reference books, university entrance guides and collections of challenging problems.

He was born on 1 January 1909 in St Petersburg and grew up in an orphanage.

## Education and Teaching

In 1928 he graduated with distinction from a veterinary technical college. He subsequently graduated from the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics at Leningrad University.

While still a student, he was recommended by Academician I. M. Vinogradov for an assistant’s position in the Department of Higher Mathematics at the Leningrad Polytechnic Institute. After graduating from university, he continued to work in the institute’s mathematics department from 1932 to 1942.

## War and the Return to Teaching

He served in the Great Patriotic War from 1942 to 1945, having volunteered for the front from besieged Leningrad.

He taught in the mathematics departments of the Leningrad Electrotechnical Institute from 1945 to 1957, at the Soviet Navy’s Higher Naval Engineering School, and at the Leningrad Polytechnic Institute from 1957 to 1984.

## Books and a Personal Recollection

Soviet pupils and teachers knew K. U. Shakhno as the author of mathematics textbooks and collections: reference books, guides for university applicants and challenging problem books. His books had a total print run of about three million copies in the USSR.

Such books were needed in Soviet times for preparing for university entrance examinations. Similar guides are now published to help pupils prepare for Russia’s Unified State Examination.

P.S. I myself used Shakhno’s *Collection of Challenging Problems in Elementary Mathematics* between 1963 and 1967 to prepare for regional mathematics olympiads and for admission to the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics at Kazan University.

Textbooks, problem collections and reference books written by K. U. Shakhno:

- *Collection of Competitive Mathematics Problems with Solutions: A Study Aid*. Leningrad, 1951.

- *Reference Book on Elementary Mathematics*. Leningrad, 1955.
- *Mathematics Reference Book: A Guide for Pupils in Grades 8–10*. Leningrad, 1957.
- *Mathematics Guide for Applicants to Higher Education: A Collection of Competitive Mathematics Problems with Solutions*. Minsk, 1960.
- *How to Prepare for University Entrance Examinations: Mathematics*. Leningrad, 1961.
- *Elements of the Theory of Functions of a Complex Variable and Operational Calculus*. Leningrad, 1961.
- *Collection of Challenging Problems in Elementary Mathematics*. Minsk: Vysshaya Shkola, 1963.
- *Elementary Mathematics for Secondary School Graduates: A Study Aid*. Leningrad, 1976.
