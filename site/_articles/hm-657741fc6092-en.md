Two people are flying in a hot-air balloon. They have drifted off course and do not know where they are. Passing a hill with a man sitting on it, they ask:<br>
“Excuse us, where are we?”

The man on the hill thinks for a long time, then replies:<br>
“In a hot-air balloon.”

One balloonist, an elderly man, says to the other:<br>
“That man on the hill is a mathematician.”<br>
“Why?”<br>
“He thought for a long time about a simple question, then gave an absolutely precise and completely useless answer…”
