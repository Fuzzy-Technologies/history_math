Many mathematicians believe that the intersection of two flat jokes is one subtle joke. Here’s one to start your Sunday.

A mathematician comes home with a bouquet of red roses and gives it to his wife, also a mathematician, saying:<br>
“I love you!”<br>
But she hits him over the head with the bouquet, then throws it in the bin. Why?

Answer: He should have said, “I love you, and only you!”
