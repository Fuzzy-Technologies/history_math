Hungry after a hunt, a hunter asked two shepherds for something to eat. After a brief discussion, they invited him to join their lunch. One shepherd had three dishes of food, and the other had two. Everyone ate an equal share, and at the end of the meal the hunter gave them 50 kopecks and left.

The shepherds began to divide the money but could not agree. They had to call the hunter back. When he heard what the argument was about, he divided the 50 kopecks so that each shepherd received the share due to him. How did he divide it?

## Solution

The hunter ate one third of the entire lunch and paid 50 kopecks for it, so the whole meal, five dishes, was worth 1 rouble 50 kopecks. Each dish therefore cost 30 kopecks. The shepherd who brought three dishes, worth 90 kopecks, ate 50 kopecks’ worth himself, so he should receive 40 kopecks from the hunter. The second shepherd brought two dishes, worth 60 kopecks, and also ate 50 kopecks’ worth, so he should receive 10 kopecks.
