Some lectures send students away with formulas. Others send them away with a phrase they will repeat for years.

One such “lecturer’s gem” goes like this:

“Picturing four-dimensional space is quite simple. All you need to do is imagine four orthonormal vectors. The rest will follow.”

Indeed: the hardest thing about four-dimensional space is not the space itself. It is honestly imagining four orthonormal vectors, without asking unnecessary questions.
