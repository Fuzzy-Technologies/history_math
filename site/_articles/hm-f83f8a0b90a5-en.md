Soon after its publication in 1865, Lewis Carroll’s *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland* came into the hands of the Queen of England. Delighted by Alice’s astonishing adventures, she immediately demanded the other books by such a remarkable writer. Imagine her disappointment when she discovered that this author’s other works were devoted to… mathematics.
