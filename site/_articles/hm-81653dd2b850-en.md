A friend’s child was given this puzzle before starting kindergarten and solved it without trouble. My friend also managed it, because she was in tune with the way a child sees the world. Want to test how much your habits shape what you see?

Here is the puzzle:

$8809=6$<br>
$7111=0$<br>
$2172=0$<br>
$6666=4$<br>
$1111=0$<br>
$3213=0$<br>
$7662=2$<br>
$9312=1$<br>
$0000=4$<br>
$2222=0$<br>
$3333=0$<br>
$5555=0$<br>
$8193=3$<br>
$8096=5$<br>
$7777=0$<br>
$9999=4$<br>
$7756=1$<br>
$6855=3$<br>
$9881=5$<br>
$5531=0$<br>
<br>
$2581=?$

## Solution

Answer: 2—the number of closed loops in the written number.
