Sometimes the most important thing in a lecture is the question, rather than the mathematics.

A student asks the lecturer:

“Is $\pi$ even or odd?”

Without a moment’s thought, the lecturer replies:

“Even, of course. $\pi$ is 180 degrees!”

The moral: ask a silly question, get a silly answer.

P. S. Just to be clear, $\pi$ is neither even nor odd. It is irrational (and transcendental). The terms even and odd apply only to integers.
