*Marking the 85th anniversary of his birth*

> “MSU is my life; it is my home.”

We congratulate Viktor Antonovich Sadovnichy on his 85th birthday. His is the best-known name in Russian education. We honour him as someone who has done a great deal for both mathematics as a science and mathematics education.

Viktor Antonovich Sadovnichy is a well-known Soviet and Russian mathematician. He has been Rector of Lomonosov Moscow State University since 1992. He received his Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences degree in 1974 and became an MSU professor in 1975 and a full member of the Russian Academy of Sciences in 1997. He was Vice-President of the Academy in 2008–2013 and is an honorary member of the Russian Academy of Education and a member of its Board of Trustees.

## Education and Research

He was born on 3 April 1939 in Krasnopavlovka, Lozova District, Kharkiv Region, into a working and farming family. He completed a village school and worked for a time at the Komsomolets mine in Horlivka, Donetsk Region.

From then on, his whole life was connected with Moscow University. He studied mathematics at MSU’s Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics in 1958–1963, graduating with honours. He defended his Candidate of Sciences dissertation, “Regularized Sums of Eigenvalues of General Problems for Ordinary Differential Equations”, in 1967, and his doctoral dissertation, “On Some Questions in the Theory of Ordinary Differential Equations Depending on a Spectral Parameter”, in 1974.

Sadovnichy is a well-known specialist in mathematics, mechanics and computer science. His principal research areas are differential equations, functional analysis, mathematical modelling, mathematical methods of information processing and mathematical theories of complex systems.

Under his scientific direction, mathematical methods for processing space data were developed that substantially advanced the interpretation and pattern recognition of satellite imagery. His distinctive mathematical software for simulators made it possible, for the first time in world spaceflight practice, to carry out end-to-end simulation of every stage of an aerospace flight.

## University Career

He progressed through every level of university teaching, from assistant to professor, and held many leadership posts at MSU. He headed the Department of Functional Analysis and Its Applications in the Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics in 1981–1982, and has headed the Department of Mathematical Analysis in the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics since 1982. His posts have included deputy dean of the Faculty for research, deputy pro-rector, pro-rector in 1982–1984 and first pro-rector in 1984–1992.

He was elected Rector of MSU on 23 March 1992 and has led the University continuously since then, being re-elected in 1996, 2001 and 2005. During this period the number of MSU faculties more than doubled. New faculties included Fundamental Medicine (1992), Public Administration (1993), Arts (2001), Bioengineering and Bioinformatics (2002), World Politics (2003), Physics and Chemistry (2006, now the Faculty of Fundamental Physical and Chemical Engineering), Political Science (2008), Biotechnology (2013) and Space Research (2017).

## Development of the University

Under his leadership, extensive construction beyond Lomonosovsky Prospekt doubled the size of the MSU campus. The Fundamental Library, Shuvalov and Lomonosov buildings, Medical Research and Education Centre, University Gymnasium and student accommodation were built. Sadovnichy initiated and leads the innovative research project Sparrow Hills Science and Technology Valley.

Branches were opened in Russia at Sevastopol, Grozny, Sarov and Dubna, and abroad at Astana, Baku, Yerevan, Tashkent and Dushanbe. Shenzhen MSU-BIT University, a joint Russian–Chinese university, opened in Shenzhen.

Thanks to his efforts, MSU remains a recognized leader among Russian higher education institutions and one of the world’s best universities.

Sadovnichy received the USSR State Prize in 1989, the Russian Federation State Prize in 2002 and three Russian Government Prizes in 2006, 2011 and 2012. He holds all classes of the Order for Merit to the Fatherland and was named a Hero of Labour of the Russian Federation in 2024.
