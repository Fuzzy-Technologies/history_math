The well-known Soviet specialist in mathematics teaching, Professor V. M. Bradis (1890–1975), once visited a school. The class was studying logarithms, and copies of Bradis’s *Four-Figure Mathematical Tables* lay on the desks. After the lesson the professor asked, “Tell me, please, who is Bradis?” Without pausing to think, one of the girls blurted out, “Oh, some ancient Greek!”
