A philosopher, a physicist and a mathematician were given the same task: dive from a platform into a pool one metre in diameter.

The philosopher concentrated, called Socrates and Hegel to mind, trusted to fate, jumped… and missed.

The physicist was thorough: he measured the height of the platform, estimated the speed and direction of the wind, did the calculations, jumped… and landed in the pool.

The mathematician was thorough too: he built a model, spent ages calculating, wrote a program, obtained a trajectory, took a run-up, jumped… and flew upwards.

A sign error…
