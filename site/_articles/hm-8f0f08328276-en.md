Mathematicians joke — sometimes rather boldly.

According to [Engelbert L. Schucking’s account in *Physics Today*](https://physicstoday.aip.org/features/jordan-pauli-politics-brecht-and-a-variable-gravitational-constant), relaying a story told by Wolfgang Pauli, Ernst Zermelo posed this problem in a logic lecture at Göttingen: “All mathematicians in Göttingen belong to two classes. Some do what they dislike but Klein likes. Others do what they like but Klein dislikes. To which class does Klein himself belong?” When the audience could not answer, Zermelo explained: “Klein is not a mathematician.”
