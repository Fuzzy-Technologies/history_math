Any number of people can play. Sit in a circle and take turns clockwise to count the natural numbers in order. Instead of a number divisible by 3 or ending in 3, say “hop” and skip that number. Anyone who makes a mistake is out, and the others continue. The last player remaining wins.

Choose one leader to check the rules and keep up the pace. Give the winner a prize. It is more fun with music! 🤓🎵🎶
