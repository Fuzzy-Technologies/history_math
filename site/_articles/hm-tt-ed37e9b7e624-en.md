A diameter gauge and centre finder is an instrument for measuring the diameters of cylindrical cross-sections, marking diameters on workpieces and finding the centres of circles and circular regions.

One design consists of a right angle and a ruler placed along its bisector, with millimetre graduations on the ruler.

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-2" %}

To measure the diameter of a workpiece's cross-section, place it between the sides of the right angle. The diameter MN is read along the bisector.

If sides AB and BC are graduated, as in the diagram above, the diameter is BK + BL, where K and L are the points at which the sides touch the circle. If needed, draw segment MN, which is a diameter of the cross-section.

To find the centre, use the instrument in two positions. Draw the bisector of the right angle in each position. The intersection of the two bisectors is the centre of the cross-section.

A modern construction square can also be used in practice as a diameter gauge and centre finder.
