*Marking the 130th anniversary of his birth*

A Soviet mathematics educator and specialist in teaching methods. He held the degree of Candidate of Pedagogical Sciences.

## From Schools to Research

He was born on 25 January (8 February) 1894.

He graduated from the Tiflis Teachers’ Institute in 1918.

He began his educational career in 1918 in the Stavropol area as head of a municipal department of public education. In the Kuban region he worked as a mathematics teacher and headteacher, as an inspector in a regional education department and as a mathematics lecturer at Stavropol Pedagogical Technical College in 1926–1931.

In 1931 he entered postgraduate study at the Research Institute for Curriculum and Teaching Methods. He subsequently remained at the institute as a senior researcher.

He taught mathematics at the All-Union Communist Institute of Journalism in 1932–1933 and Moscow City Pedagogical Institute in 1933–1941.

In the 1930s he worked with E. S. Berezanskaya on questions of recording and assessing school attainment.

## Wartime Work and the Moscow Pedagogical Institute

During the Great Patriotic War of 1941–1945, he worked as a senior researcher at the Institute of Schools of the Russian SFSR’s People’s Commissariat of Education, from 1941 to 1943.

From 1945 to 1980, Prochuhaev was an associate professor in the Department of Mathematics Teaching Methods at the V. I. Lenin Moscow State Pedagogical Institute. He was dean of its Faculty of Physics and Mathematics in 1951–1956.

Prochuhaev received the Orders of Lenin and the Red Banner of Labour, the Medal for Valiant Labour in the Great Patriotic War of 1941–1945 and the K. D. Ushinsky Medal.

## Notes

He wrote about forty scholarly works on teaching methods, addressing polytechnical education, connections between theory and practice in mathematics teaching, the development of pupils’ calculation, graphing and measurement skills, and improvements in teaching and mathematical attainment. Some of these works are listed below; the Russian titles are translated here.

- V. G. Prochuhaev, *Measures of Attainment in the Topic “Common Fractions”*. Dissertation, Central Research Institute of Polytechnical Education, 1935.

- E. S. Berezanskaya, E. D. Zagoskina and V. G. Prochuhaev, “On Examinations in Mathematics”, *Mathematics at School*, 1939, no. 3, pp. 51–57.

The following useful book has become a bibliographic rarity:

- V. G. Prochuhaev, *Measurements in the Secondary-School Mathematics Course*. Moscow: Prosveshchenie, 1965. 140 pp.
