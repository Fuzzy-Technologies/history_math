Billion = $10^9$<br>
Trillion = $10^{12}$<br>
Quadrillion = $10^{15}$

What is an octillion?

## Solution

Answer: $10^{27}$. We are interested not only in the answer, but in why it works. In the context of this problem, the answer is 10 to the 27th power. The pattern comes from the Latin names for the consecutive numbers 2, 3, 4, …

## Notes

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-2" %}

Historical background. In 1484, the French mathematician and physician Nicolas Chuquet extended names based on “million” as far as nonillion in *The Science of Numbers in Three Parts*. Chuquet used the long scale: each successive term denoted the next power of a million, so billion corresponded to $10^{12}$. The problem above uses the modern short scale, in which billion is $10^9$ and octillion is $10^{27}$.
