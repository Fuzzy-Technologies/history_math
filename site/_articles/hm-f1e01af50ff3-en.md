Napoleon Bonaparte supported the exact sciences and even appointed several prominent scholars to high office. For example, he made the great mathematician Laplace (1749–1827) Minister of the Interior. After a month and a half, however, Laplace was removed: while attending to every trivial detail, he had overlooked a major conspiracy against the state. Napoleon’s order read: “Dismiss him for introducing the spirit of infinitesimals into affairs of state.”
