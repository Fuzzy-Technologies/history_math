Boris Nikolayevich Dolgov (1894–1959), a chemist, poet and professor at Leningrad State University, wrote these humorous verses about its branch in Yelabuga, where he worked.

In 1925, B. N. Dolgov graduated from the Chemistry Division of the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics at Leningrad University.

Fate has made ready for us<br>
Yet another tale of the sea.

There are no brigs or pirates here,<br>
But our storyteller will tell<br>
Of the perilous, thorny road<br>
From Yelabuga to Saratov.

As in an adventure novel,<br>
Our tale begins far away.<br>
For now, let us travel<br>
To the hills and skies of Tatarstan.

In remote Yelabuga on the Kama,<br>
Curved like an integral sign,<br>
The Leningrad branch was known<br>
Even beyond Chelny.

Its staff ploughed the fields,<br>
Milked goats and tossed hay into stacks,<br>
And over two years, as the days flew by,<br>
Their clothes grew ever more threadbare.
