*Marking the 115th anniversary of his birth*

A Soviet mathematics educator and associate professor. He wrote school textbooks, guides to teaching methods and specialist courses for teacher-training institutions.

His textbooks *A Special Course in Elementary Algebra* and *A Special Course in Trigonometry* became particularly well known and proved valuable in preparing secondary-school mathematics teachers. These are translations of the Russian titles.

## Education and Teaching

He was born on 4 January 1909.

He graduated from Moscow State University in 1930. He was a student of N. N. Luzin, D. F. Egorov and I. I. Privalov.

He taught at the Moscow Electromechanical Engineering Institute from 1929 and in the Faculty of Physics at Moscow State University from 1931. From 1966 he taught in I. K. Andronov’s department at the N. K. Krupskaya Moscow Regional Pedagogical Institute.

He was deputy editor-in-chief of the journal *Mathematics at School* from 1940 to 1960 and published numerous articles on mathematics and teaching methods in it.

## Textbooks and Specialist Courses

He wrote a school trigonometry textbook and specialist courses in elementary mathematics for teacher-training institutions. The following Russian titles are translated here:

- (1950) S. I. Novoselov, *Inverse Trigonometric Functions: A Guide for Teachers*.
- (1953) S. I. Novoselov, *A Special Course in Elementary Algebra: For Pedagogical Institutes*.
- (1953) M. K. Grebencha and S. I. Novoselov, *A Course in Mathematical Analysis*.
- (1958) S. I. Novoselov, *A Guide to Teaching Trigonometry: A Handbook for Teachers*.
- (1964) S. I. Novoselov, *Trigonometry: A Textbook for Grades 9–10 of Secondary School*.
- (1966) S. I. Novoselov and P. S. Modenov, *Mathematics: A Guide for University Applicants*.
- (1967) S. I. Novoselov, *A Special Course in Trigonometry: A Textbook for Pedagogical Institutes and State Universities*.
