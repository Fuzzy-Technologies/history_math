The Soviet mathematician Dmitry Yevgenyevich Menshov (1892–1988) was a legendary figure among his colleagues. He was completely absorbed in mathematics, which gave his whole life its meaning. Besides his scientific legacy, he therefore left his colleagues with many stories that showed what an unusual person he was.

Once he was asked to describe the birth of the Moscow school of mathematics. V. M. Tikhomirov, who was there, recalled Menshov’s account: “In 1914 I entered Moscow University. That year, Dmitry Fyodorovich Yegorov and Nikolai Nikolayevich Luzin organised a seminar for students on numerical series. In 1915 we studied series of functions, and in 1916, orthogonal series. Then came 1917. What a memorable year that was in our lives! Something of the greatest importance happened that year, shaping all the rest of our lives: we began studying trigonometric series!”

## Notes

{% include article-figure.html id="fig-2" %}

[Dmitry Yevgenyevich Menshov](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9C%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%8C%D1%88%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%94%D0%BC%D0%B8%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%95%D0%B2%D0%B3%D0%B5%D0%BD%D1%8C%D0%B5%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87) (1892–1988) was a Soviet mathematician, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, professor at Moscow State University and corresponding member of the USSR Academy of Sciences. He obtained fundamental results and published many works on trigonometric series.

In Tikhomirov’s account, Menshov gives 1914 as the year he entered university. In his published recollections, Menshov dates his enrolment to the autumn of 1912. The seminar began in 1914. [Tikhomirov’s account in Kvant, no. 4, 1996, p. 24](https://www.kvant.digital/issues/1996/4/tihomirov-o_matematikah_s_ulyibkoy-7a0fd755/) · [Menshov’s recollections](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~neretin/misc/luzin/menshov.html) · [Moscow State University biography](https://new.math.msu.su/tffa/memory/menshov.html)
