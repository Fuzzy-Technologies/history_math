Today mathematicians are poking fun at engineers. ⚙️

The English mechanical engineer and physicist Osborne Reynolds (1842–1912), known for his research in fluid dynamics, was once explaining how to use a slide rule to first-year students.

“Let’s take a simple example,” he said. “$3 \times 4$.”

After explaining and demonstrating the calculation on a large model of the slide rule, he continued:

“And here is the result: $3 \times 4$ is… is $11.8$. For our purposes,” Reynolds added without missing a beat, “that accuracy is quite sufficient!”
