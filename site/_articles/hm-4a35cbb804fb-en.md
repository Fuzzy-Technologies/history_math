*Marking the 100th anniversary of his birth*

A Soviet mathematician and educator, and an organizer of scientific research and mathematics education. He wrote textbooks and popular science books for school pupils.

He was born on 18 June 1924 in the village of Suvo in the Buryat-Mongol Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic.

## Education and the Front

After finishing school, he entered the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics at Irkutsk University.

Six months later he was drafted into the army and sent to the Transbaikal Infantry School.

Lieutenant Likhtarnikov began serving at the front in 1942.

In 1946 he was demobilized and returned to Irkutsk to continue his studies.

## Research and Teaching

His research and teaching career began in 1950 at Irkutsk University, where he worked until 1959. He then worked at Khabarovsk Pedagogical Institute in 1959–1971. In 1954 he defended his Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences dissertation, “Linear Integral Equations with Two Parameters”.

From 1971 Likhtarnikov worked at Novgorod State Pedagogical Institute as an associate professor in the Department of Mathematics and as head of the Department of Mathematical Analysis.

Until his retirement in 1994, he combined research and teaching at the institute with public work. He served on the mathematics curriculum commission of the Russian SFSR’s Ministry of Education and the editorial board of *Differentsirovannye uravneniya*, a republic-wide collection of papers from mathematics departments at pedagogical institutes of the Russian SFSR. For twenty years he chaired the presidium of the regional branch of the Pedagogical Society of the Russian SFSR.

His principal research concerned mathematical analysis and the theory of linear integral equations. He also wrote a two-volume university textbook on mathematical logic.

## Honours and Emigration

For his service in the Great Patriotic War of 1941–1945, Likhtarnikov received the Order of the Red Star and medals. His research and teaching were recognized with the badges “Excellence in Public Education of the Russian SFSR” and “Excellence in Education of the USSR”.

In 1998 he and his wife moved to the United States to join their daughter in Rochester.

## Notes

He wrote textbooks and popular science books for school pupils, including the following (Russian titles translated here):

- *Logical Puzzles* (1995).
- *Puzzles of the Sages* (1996).
- *Cryptarithms and How to Solve Them* (1996).
- *An Elementary Introduction to Functional Equations* (1997).
