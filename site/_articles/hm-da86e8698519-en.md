L is for Logic! Today’s joke about logic features a crocodile that doesn’t exist. 🐊

## Theorem 1. A crocodile is longer than it is wide

### Proof

Split the theorem into two lemmas.

Lemma 1. A crocodile is longer than it is green.

Proof. A crocodile is long both on top and underneath, but green only on top.

Lemma 2. A crocodile is greener than it is wide.

Proof. A crocodile is green both lengthwise and widthwise, but wide only widthwise.

Lemmas 1 and 2 $\Rightarrow$ a crocodile is longer than it is wide.

However, the opposite theorem can be proved in the same way.

## Theorem 2. A crocodile is wider than it is long

### Proof

Lemma 1. A crocodile is wider than it is green.

Proof. A crocodile is wide both on top and underneath, but green only on top.

Lemma 2. A crocodile is greener than it is long.

Proof. A crocodile is green both lengthwise and widthwise, but long only lengthwise.

Lemmas 1 and 2 $\Rightarrow$ a crocodile is wider than it is long.

## Conclusion

These two theorems might suggest that a crocodile is square. But that is incorrect, because all the inequalities are strict. Therefore, the crocodile does not exist!
