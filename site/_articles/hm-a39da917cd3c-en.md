Mathematicians tease physicists: the standard of horsepower has been kept at the Main Chamber of Weights and Measures ever since D. I. Mendeleev’s day!

🐎 One horsepower is the force of a horse one metre tall and weighing 1 kg.

P.S. The great Russian polymath Dmitry Ivanovich Mendeleev became scientific custodian of the Depot of Standard Weights and Measures in 1892. In 1893 he took charge of its successor, the [Main Chamber of Weights and Measures](https://www.vniim.ru/about/history/head_of_vniim/mendeleev-dmitriy-ivanovich/) in St Petersburg. He did not invent vodka, as some people think! 😄
