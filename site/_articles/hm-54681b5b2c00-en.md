“All is number,” said Pythagoras, explaining every phenomenon in terms of numbers. Yet some numbers were less welcome than others.

The Pythagoreans disliked a certain integer because it lay between two integers, each of which is the area of a rectangle whose perimeter has the same numerical value. Find the number.

## Solution

Answer: 17. It lies between 16 and 18. A square with side 4 has both area and perimeter 16. A rectangle with sides 3 and 6 has both area and perimeter 18.

Historical background. What was bad for Pythagoras was good for Gauss. In 1796, shortly before his nineteenth birthday, Gauss made his famous discovery that a regular 17-gon can be constructed with compass and straightedge. A regular 17-pointed star appears on the monument to Gauss in Braunschweig.
