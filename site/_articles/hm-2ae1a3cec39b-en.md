A village school has as many boys as girls. One day the teacher brought 234 nuts to class and shared them out, giving each boy 5 nuts and each girl 4. The girls were upset by this unfairness, so the teacher had to bring in more nuts and distribute them so that everyone eventually had the same number: 6 nuts each. How many nuts did the teacher bring the second time?

## Solution

The first time, one boy and one girl received 9 nuts between them, so there were 26 boys and 26 girls in the school ($234 / 9$), making 52 pupils in all ($26 \times 2$). The teacher therefore brought a total of 312 nuts on the two occasions ($52 \times 6$), so the second batch contained 78 nuts ($312 - 234$).
