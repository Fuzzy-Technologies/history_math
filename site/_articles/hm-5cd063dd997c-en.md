📢 A group of mathematicians has taken a self-styled guru hostage after a bold claim! 💥

Today a local spiritual guru found himself at the centre of a scientific scandal after declaring that “all knowledge is already within us”. 🧘‍♂️💫 Armed with textbooks and coffee, a group of mathematicians decided to test that theory in practice! ☕📚

## What happened?

- The guru was locked in a lecture room with a board full of differential equations!
- The demand: “Meditate until you solve them!” 😤🧘‍♂️✍️
- According to preliminary reports, the guru has already passed through every stage of enlightenment, from “Om” to “Oh, what’s that integral sign?!” He has realised that “nirvana” is just a special case and learnt to run faster than the gradient! 🤯📉

“If he hasn’t derived the general solution by tomorrow morning, we’ll bring in a Taylor series! Then we’ll differentiate and integrate him!” one scientist declared, rubbing his hands ominously.

😆📢 Stay tuned! The world may soon see its first enlightened mathematician…
