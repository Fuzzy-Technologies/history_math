Ivan Alekseyevich Vyshnegradsky (1831–1895), an honorary member of the Academy of Sciences and Minister of Finance, had been director of the St Petersburg Technological Institute. According to a familiar anecdote, he often scolded professors for the many errors in their lithographed lecture notes and held up his own as an example. Yet the title of his own notes gave him away. The initial sounds of *zhivykh sil* in the title *Teoriya zhivykh sil* (“The Theory of Living Forces”) had traded places, so the notes read *Teoriya sivykh zhil*, or “The Theory of Grey Veins”.
