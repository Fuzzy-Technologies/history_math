You can perform this trick with one person or a group.

Ask the participant — or everyone in the group — to think of a positive whole number with several digits, then do the following:

1. Write down the number.

2. Rearrange the digits to make a different number. If all the digits are the same, choose a different starting number.

3. Subtract the smaller number from the larger. If the difference contains no digit from 1 to 8, try a different arrangement or start with another number.

4. Cross out one digit of the difference, other than 0 or 9.

5. Add up the remaining digits.

6. Say the resulting sum aloud.

From that sum, you will name the crossed-out digit.

How does it work? What is the secret?

## The secret

The secret of nine: the trick rests on the divisibility test for 9. The two numbers have the same digit sum and therefore the same remainder when divided by 9. Their difference is thus divisible by 9. The crossed-out digit is what you must add to the stated sum to reach the next greater multiple of 9.

## Notes

If you allow any digit to be crossed out, the sum of those left may itself be divisible by 9. You must then say the missing digit is either 0 or 9: it cannot be determined uniquely.
