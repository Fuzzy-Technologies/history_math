# Mathematics with Mansur

<img src="site/assets/images/Math-with-Mansur.png" alt="Math with Mansur" width="100%">

An illustrated online journal exploring the history of mathematics: the people, ideas, discoveries and problems that shaped the discipline.

Edited by Mansur Gilmullin, the journal brings together historical essays, mathematical explorations and problems for curious readers. Articles are available in English and Russian, with downloadable PDF editions.
