# Article publication details

The native `details` disclosure near the article heading separates the website publication date from the documented earlier publication. It works without JavaScript. It includes authorship, journal name, language, publication/update dates when present and the canonical article URL. Drafts and demos never receive a fabricated final citation. Translation notices are pages, not translated articles, and do not receive article citation metadata.

Russian website references follow [GOST R 7.0.108-2022](https://sziu-lib.ranepa.ru/data/gost7.0.108-2022.pdf), sections 4.2-4.3, 5.1-5.3 and 6.1: cite this electronic version, use known publication data, retain its URL and access date. The access date is the reader's current local day, recalculated on each page load or refresh without a calendar control; without JavaScript its placeholder is explicit. A website citation has no invented volume, issue, page range, DOI or ISSN. Visible clickable URLs are intentional in the citation block because the reference must be portable; article prose still uses descriptive links.

English references use [APA 7, webpage on a website with an individual author](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/webpage-website-references): surname and initials, the content publication/update date, italicized page title, website name and URL. An ordinary stable article needs no retrieval date. The title is taken verbatim from the English article; acronyms and proper names are not lowercased automatically.

`site/_data/citation_authors.json` records verified author name forms. Mansur's M. F. initials occur in the existing author/book caption in the perfect-numbers package. Add a verified form for a new personal author; never infer family names or initials from word order. Unregistered names remain verbatim (also supporting organizational names). Multiple authors preserve their source order; GOST uses a title-led entry for four or more authors, and APA uses its 20-author limit. No author name is translated by machine during a build.

The copy button copies plain text and falls back to selection when clipboard access is unavailable. The DOI/ISSN fields are deliberately absent until actual identifiers have been supplied. Registering a journal or receiving a DOI is independent of formatting a reference.
