# Article publication details

The native `details` disclosure near the article heading separates the website publication date from the documented earlier publication. It works without JavaScript. It includes authorship, journal name, language, publication/update dates when present and the canonical article URL. Archive citation URLs include the stable work hash and language suffix; see [Stable article URLs](ARTICLE_URLS.md). Drafts and demos never receive a fabricated final citation. Translation notices are pages, not translated articles, and do not receive article citation metadata.

Russian website references follow [GOST R 7.0.108-2022](https://sziu-lib.ranepa.ru/data/gost7.0.108-2022.pdf), sections 4.2-4.3, 5.1-5.3 and 6.1: cite this electronic version, use known publication data, retain its URL and access date. The access date is the reader's current local day, recalculated on each page load or refresh without a calendar control; without JavaScript its placeholder is explicit. A website citation has no invented volume, issue, page range, DOI or ISSN. Visible clickable URLs are intentional in the citation block because the reference must be portable; article prose still uses descriptive links.

English references follow the **Periodicals Online** format in the [IEEE Reference Guide](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/IEEE_Reference_Guide.pdf): initials before surname, quoted article title, italicized journal title, website publication date, access date and `[Online]. Available:` followed by the canonical URL. The access date uses the reader's current local day in English and is recalculated on page load; without JavaScript, an explicit date placeholder remains. Updates are listed separately in the publication details and do not replace the publication date in the reference. No volume, issue, page range or DOI is invented. The full journal name is retained because no official abbreviation is registered. The reference number belongs to the citing document's bibliography and is not assigned by the copy button. Article titles retain their supplied spelling and proper names.

`site/_data/citation_authors.json` records verified author name forms. Mansur's M. F. initials occur in the existing author/book caption in the perfect-numbers package. Add a verified form for a new personal author; never infer family names or initials from word order. Unregistered names remain verbatim (also supporting organizational names). Multiple authors preserve their source order; GOST uses a title-led entry for four or more authors. IEEE lists up to six authors, joins the last with `and`, and uses the first author followed by `et al.` for seven or more. No author name is translated by machine during a build.

The copy button copies plain text and falls back to selection when clipboard access is unavailable. The DOI/ISSN fields are deliberately absent until actual identifiers have been supplied. Registering a journal or receiving a DOI is independent of formatting a reference.
